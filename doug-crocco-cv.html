Doug Crocco
Artist and curator · Los Angeles, California
doug@dougcrocco.com · dougcrocco.com
Doug Crocco is an artist and curator based in Los Angeles, California. He has exhibited in New York, Los Angeles, Palm Beach, Miami, Boston, and Melbourne. He received his MFA from Claremont Graduate University in 2004. From 2015 to 2020 he was owner and director of the project space Big Pictures Los Angeles. He is currently producing new artworks, and spending time in Mexico City working on new projects.

Big Pictures Los Angeles was a project space in mid-city Los Angeles, founded in 2014 to show underserved mid-career artists, present new forms of digital media, and collaborate with artists in Miami and New York. It closed in 2020; the program carries on as curatorial projects at spaces around the world.
